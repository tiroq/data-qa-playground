# Session 16 — Experience Stories — Evidence-Based Interview Answers

**Date (Asia/Bangkok):** 2026-11-14 · **Duration:** 60 minutes · **Status:** PLANNED  
**Track:** Exness QA Engineer (Data Stack) interview preparation

> **Language contract:** The actual technical interview is in **Russian**. All live tasks, discussion, follow-up questions, code review, mock interviews, and feedback in ChatGPT must be **in Russian**. SQL, Python, identifiers, and technical terms may remain in English. This repository's written exercise specifications are in English.

## Goal

Present authentic, concise Russian-language stories tied to Data Stack QA.

## Practical assignment

1. Prepare three 2–3 minute STAR stories: Reslab reconciliation/smoke quality, NATS JetStream/ClickHouse work and QA automation/CI.
2. State your actual role, technical constraints, decisions, outcome and what you would improve.
3. Expect skeptical follow-ups about metrics, ownership and validation methodology.
4. Map each story to SQL, data correctness, streaming or test architecture.

## Contracts and constraints

- Do not invent metrics, employers' internal facts or unverified performance improvements.
- Keep the story accurate and bounded to personal contributions.
- English may appear in technology and code names, not as a speaking requirement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each story fits in 2–3 minutes.
- [ ] One trade-off, one measurable or verifiable outcome (if available), one lesson.
- [ ] Answers withstand at least two follow-up questions.
